Neighbors, Not Strangers

We built this neighborhood. Let's govern it too.

For over a century, Acres Homes has been home — tree-lined streets, big yards, family land passed down for generations, and neighbors who look out for one another. Now we're coming together to incorporate as our own city, so the people who live here and pay taxes here have the final say over our streets, our services, and our future.

Why this matters

Acres Homes built itself. It's time to govern itself.

Acres Homes was founded by Black families who bought land in acre-sized lots when they were shut out of housing elsewhere in Houston — land large enough to raise gardens, keep animals, and build generational wealth. That same spirit of independence is why residents are organizing today.

1Local Control

City council members elected by and accountable directly to Acres Homes residents — not one voice among many in a city of over two million people.

2Our Tax Dollars, Our Streets

Property and sales tax revenue generated in Acres Homes would be budgeted for Acres Homes — roads, drainage, parks, and public safety, set as local priorities.

3A Proven Path

Communities across the Houston area — from Bellaire to Missouri City — have incorporated and self-governed for decades. We can too.
